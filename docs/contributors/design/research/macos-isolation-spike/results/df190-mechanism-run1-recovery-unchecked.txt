== D4 RECOVERY — is the displaced sandbox recoverable, and by what? ==
        An unrecoverable state is a different severity from a self-healing one, and the difference
        decides whether the product can paper over this or has to avoid causing it.
        after a further 10s with no intervention: W -> 1.1.1.1 = 000
        W after a stop/start: state=running  addr=192.168.66.3  gw=192.168.66.1  host bridge=bridge101
        after stop/start: W -> 1.1.1.1 = 301
   PASS    D4: it does NOT self-heal but a stop/start recovers it. So the sandbox is salvageable and the
            cost is a restart of an innocent workload — which for an agent mid-task is not free.
