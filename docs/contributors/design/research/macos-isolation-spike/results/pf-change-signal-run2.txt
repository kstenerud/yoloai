== S5 THE COST OF POLLING, since that is what a negative forces ==
        20 invocations of the granted read, as the user would issue them:
        pfctl -s info x20 (as root, no sudo): 2.0 ms each
        pfctl -s rules x20 (the main-ruleset read a poll would need): 4.2 ms each
        
        Add ~9.3 ms of sudo per call (pf-acquire-cost.txt, where 85% of a call was sudo and not pfctl).
        At a 5s interval that is a fraction of a percent of one core, and a 5s worst-case window of
        unenforced running. The interval is the design parameter; these are its two axes.
