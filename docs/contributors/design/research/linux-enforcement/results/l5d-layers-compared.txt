=== verdict ===
Read the sibling column in the two 'ALONE' rows. The host layer cannot act on
same-bridge sibling traffic at all — those packets are switched, not routed, and
never enter the forward hook. Whether the in-guest layer catches them is what the
second row answers, and it answers it without the host layer present to help.
