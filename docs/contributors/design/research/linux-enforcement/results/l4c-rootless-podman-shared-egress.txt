=== per-container cgroups, for contrast — these ARE distinct ===
  yb_s1 cgroup=...3c3d54f3cf35b2fa
  yb_s2 cgroup=...a451b21c80977818
  yb_s3 cgroup=...d0c030698c03282a

CONCLUSION: the sandboxes have distinct cgroups, but they do not originate the
host-visible packets. One shared egress process does, and its cgroup names the
rootless network namespace rather than any sandbox. So on rootless podman there
is no per-sandbox key at the host level: not the address (NAT'd to the host's
own), and not the cgroup (shared).
